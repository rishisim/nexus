\section*{Limitations}

\paragraph{Evidence and inference scope.}
We measure orchestration over benchmark-provided evidence, not end-to-end RAG,
complete filings, OCR, or missing evidence. All reported samples are
development evidence. Final identifiers were used only for overlap exclusion;
no final example was executed or scored, so there is no confirmatory accuracy
claim.

\paragraph{Model, policy, and scoring dependence.}
Provider behavior, latency, and prices change. V2 uses one inexpensive 2024
OpenAI model and one retrieval-first bounded ReAct policy; stronger or
differently designed agents may behave differently. Dataset-native metrics may
miss semantically valid explanations or accept normalization artifacts. The
trace audit is not human validation, and its semantic candidates remain
unresolved; v2 instead uses fresh objective-dataset items under a shared strict
contract.

\paragraph{Benchmark and system scope.}
FinDER remains secondary because it lacks human-validated objective labels.
The public English benchmarks do not represent private data, multilingual
reporting, or investment-decision safety. Query parsing, ranking, dossier
construction, and output parsing are deterministic workflow components, not
autonomous agents; our claim concerns selection between one-call Static and
bounded iterative reasoning.
