\section*{Limitations}

\paragraph{Controlled rather than end-to-end evidence.}
The study measures orchestration conditional on benchmark-provided evidence. It cannot establish how the systems retrieve from complete filings, handle OCR, or operate under missing evidence. Calling the primary result ``RAG performance'' would therefore be incorrect.

\paragraph{Narrative evaluation.}
FinanceBench and especially FinDER contain semantically valid answers that strict matching may miss. The unused confirmatory protocol specified two model-judge sensitivity analyses, but the failed development gate stopped them before execution; in any event, no human labels would validate their calibration, and judge agreement would not remove shared model bias. These datasets remain secondary and do not enter the headline macro-average.

\paragraph{Development size and selection shift.}
This manuscript reports development evidence, not a test estimate. The second-family sample was prospectively frozen but remains another development comparison. Identifier metadata from the final manifest was used only for overlap exclusion; no final example was executed or scored. The stop therefore preserves the confirmatory partition but leaves no basis for a final accuracy claim.

\paragraph{Model and price dependence.}
Provider behavior, latency, and prices change. The second-family replication uses one inexpensive 2024 OpenAI model through OpenRouter and the same prompts and scoring contract; it is not broad cross-model validation. We snapshot resolved identifiers and prices and report raw token counts alongside dollars, but the ordering may not transfer to stronger models or other deployment settings.

\paragraph{Benchmark and scoring validity.}
Dataset-native execution metrics emphasize answer correctness but may under-reward valid explanations. Conversely, tolerance, scale inference, and normalization can create false matches. The trace audit found a consequential answer-contract interaction and is not human validation; official scores remain unchanged pending author adjudication. FinDER was excluded from routing and primary quality because no human-validated objective labels were available. The five benchmarks are public English-language tasks and do not cover private data, multilingual reporting, or investment-decision safety.

\paragraph{No autonomous multi-agent claim.}
The query parser, evidence ranker, dossier builder, and output parser are deterministic workflow components. Describing each as an autonomous agent would inflate the architectural claim. Our contribution is selective orchestration between a static language-model call and bounded iterative reasoning.
