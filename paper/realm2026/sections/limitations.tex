\section*{Limitations}

\paragraph{Evidence and inference scope.}
We measure orchestration over benchmark evidence, not end-to-end RAG, complete
filings, OCR, or missing evidence. All samples are development evidence. Final
identifiers were used only for overlap exclusion; no final example was executed
or scored. V2 was frozen before inference but designed after the pilot and a
failed corrective freeze, so it is prospective correction rather than
independent preregistered confirmation.

\paragraph{Model, policy, and scoring dependence.}
V2 uses one inexpensive 2024 model and one bounded retrieval-first ReAct policy.
A prospective GPT-5.6 Luna/Terra extension stopped on action-contract failures
before a complete Luna tier or any Terra call; partial outcomes were not
analyzed. Native metrics may miss semantic validity. The trace audit is author
review, not independent human validation; adjudicating its unresolved cases
would be secondary to the cleaner fresh-sample study. The arms share retrieval
interfaces and ceilings, not dossiers, so the estimate includes retrieval-policy
effects.

\paragraph{Benchmark and system scope.}
FinDER lacks human-validated labels. Public English benchmarks omit private and
multilingual data and investment safety. Deterministic parsing and retrieval are
workflow components, not autonomous agents. Our result concerns bounded
retrieval-first ReAct over controlled evidence, not iterative agents generally;
no router is trained or evaluated on v2 outcomes.
