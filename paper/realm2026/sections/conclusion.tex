\section{Conclusion}

Across three prospectively frozen model tiers, capability changes the relative
accuracy of bounded retrieval-first ReAct: Static wins for control and Luna,
while Terra reaches parity. Yet every tier supplies at most two ReAct-only exact
successes, less than 1.4 points of oracle headroom, and ReAct costs more calls,
time, and dollars. Stronger model capability can erase an iterative workflow's
average deficit without creating useful expert complementarity. Routing should
therefore begin with a contract-fair paired feasibility audit, not a selector.
