\section{Conclusion}

Across three prospectively frozen model tiers, capability changes the relative
accuracy of bounded retrieval-first ReAct: Static wins for control and Luna,
while Terra reaches parity. Native-quality oracle-headroom point estimates are
at most 1.7 points (95\% upper limits at most 3.8); exact-match estimates are at
most 1.3 (upper limits below 4.8). A different-seed, same-sample check retains
the low-headroom pattern. Even the optimistic bounds are oracle ceilings, while
ReAct costs more calls, time, and dollars. Stronger capability can erase an
iterative workflow's average deficit without creating useful expert
complementarity. Routing should therefore begin with a contract-fair paired
feasibility audit, not a selector.
