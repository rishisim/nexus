\section{Why the Gate Failed}
\label{sec:analysis}

Router labels were available for 200 examples across FinanceBench, FinQA,
TAT-QA, and ConvFinQA. Official exact scores gave 42 Static-only successes, 10
ReAct-only successes, 37 both-correct, and 111 both-wrong. The attainable oracle
was therefore only 0.050 above always-static accuracy. Sparse benefit folds
activated the protocol's rule fallback, which escalated 70\% of cases and
reached 0.295 exact accuracy at US\$0.001431 per example, versus 0.395 for
always static.

\paragraph{Evaluation-contract audit.}
The comparison shares evidence, retrieval semantics, model binding, retries,
and a framework-blind scorer, but it is not equal-prompt or equal-context.
One-call prompts require a short final answer; ReAct only requires
\texttt{Finish[answer]}, and its growing transcript has no common dossier cap.
A deterministic post hoc audit flagged 28/52 one-sided scored-wrong outputs as
scorer-sensitive because they contained the reference value or a close form,
including all 10 ReAct-only cases. We do not change official scores or call this
human adjudication: the value may be an operand, use a wrong unit, or appear in
an invalid extra claim. The result characterizes configured workflows and
evaluation contracts, not pure reasoning ability.

\paragraph{Corrective evidence.}
The harmonized v2 addresses the two largest contract asymmetries on fresh items
excluded from earlier studies: both arms share final-answer, context-ceiling,
output, and malformed-response rules, and all ReAct rows demonstrably retrieved
before answering. They share a retrieval API and ceiling, not identical evidence
exposure: Static averaged 2.87 retrievals and ReAct 1.04. Static still had 16
more unique exact successes, and the frozen macro interval excluded zero. Thus
empty-context abstention and parser asymmetry cannot explain the v2 ordering,
but the comparison is between end-to-end retrieval policies rather than pure
reasoning over identical dossiers. It does not identify whether ReAct's errors
arise from query choice, evidence selection, arithmetic, or answer synthesis.

The routing failure follows mechanically from measured complementarity: a
broad rule cannot recover rare benefits without sending many cases to the
weaker measured branch. The v2 oracle union improves over always-Static exact
accuracy by only 3.3 points. No router is fit or evaluated on v2; its oracle is a
diagnostic upper bound. Retuning a router on these outcomes and then treating
them as evaluation would invalidate the protocol.
