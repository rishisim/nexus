\section{Related Work}

FinQA, TAT-QA, and ConvFinQA evaluate numerical reasoning over financial reports
and conversations~\cite{chen2021finqa,zhu2021tatqa,chen2022convfinqa};
FinanceBench and FinDER emphasize filing-grounded open-book or professional
queries~\cite{islam2023financebench,choi2025finder}. Our controlled-evidence
setting holds document access fixed and therefore evaluates orchestration, not
end-to-end retrieval.

ReAct interleaves reasoning with external actions~\cite{yao2023react}, while
financial agent work explores reflection and multi-stage research
workflows~\cite{fatemi2024reflection,choi2025finagentbench,sun2025finresearchbench}.
Prior studies also find diminishing or negative returns from additional
reasoning~\cite{chen2024donotthink,vamvourellis2025overthinking}. Selective
prediction and model routing optimize risk--coverage or quality--cost
trade-offs~\cite{geifman2019selectivenet,chen2024frugalgpt,ong2025routellm}.
We connect these lines by testing an often implicit prerequisite: whether the
candidate expensive expert has enough complementary accuracy to make routing
useful at all.
