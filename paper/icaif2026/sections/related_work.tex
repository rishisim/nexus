\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related-work}

\textbf{Financial QA.}
FinQA introduced expert-authored financial-report questions with executable programs; TAT-QA broadened the evidence to hybrid tables and text, and ConvFinQA added conversational dependencies~\cite{chen2021finqa,zhu2021tatqa,chen2022convfinqa}.
FinanceBench instead evaluates open-book questions over filings, and FinDER emphasizes terse professional queries paired with expert evidence for retrieval-augmented generation~\cite{islam2023financebench,choi2025finder}.
FinQAPT joins report identification, context extraction, and answering in an end-to-end pipeline~\cite{singh2024finqapt}.
Our controlled-evidence setting holds retrieval fixed to isolate reasoning orchestration.
Accordingly, FinDER and FinanceBench serve only as secondary semantic stress tests rather than being pooled with execution-scored numerical tasks.

\textbf{Agentic financial reasoning and evaluation.}
ReAct interleaves language-model reasoning with external actions~\cite{yao2023react}.
In finance, multi-agent reflection improves answers through one or more critic roles~\cite{fatemi2024reflection}, whereas FinAgentBench evaluates two-stage agentic retrieval and FinResearchBench evaluates long-form research agents with logic-tree judgments~\cite{choi2025finagentbench,sun2025finresearchbench}.
These works motivate iterative computation, but do not establish that every question benefits from it.
Indeed, both general reasoning and financial sentiment studies document overthinking or diminishing returns from extra reasoning~\cite{chen2024donotthink,vamvourellis2025overthinking}.

\textbf{Selective and cost-aware inference.}
Selective prediction formalizes risk--coverage trade-offs by declining or escalating uncertain cases~\cite{geifman2019selectivenet}.
FrugalGPT and RouteLLM learn cost-sensitive cascades or routers across models~\cite{chen2024frugalgpt,ong2025routellm}; recent agent-efficiency work likewise argues for joint effectiveness and operational-cost measurement~\cite{wang2025efficientagents,erol2025costofpass}.
We apply this principle within a fixed model and evidence budget: a pre-generation router chooses between deterministic-first, one-call adjudication and iterative ReAct.
We therefore compare quality jointly with calls, tokens, dollar cost, and latency. The unopened confirmatory protocol reserves RAGAS-style semantic judgments for a secondary, judge-sensitive analysis; the failed development gate stopped that analysis before execution~\cite{es2024ragas}.
