\section{Conclusion}

This work treated agentic financial QA as a resource-allocation hypothesis and allowed a preregistered development gate to reject the current implementation. CoT/PoT achieved the strongest development quality--cost point, while Selective Nexus was less accurate and more expensive; the fallback router escalated 70\% of the objective set yet underperformed the static path. We therefore did not open the disjoint 900-example final manifest. The current evidence does not support an ICAIF main-track selective-agency claim. Its defensible use is a workshop/protocol negative result and a scaffold for a later, independently gated redesign. Honoring that stop is itself essential: a larger test cannot repair a method that failed its prespecified development criterion without converting the untouched benchmark into another tuning set.
